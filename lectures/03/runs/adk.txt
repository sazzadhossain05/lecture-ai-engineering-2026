### What is the difference between LoRA and QLoRA?
[model] gemini-3.5-flash-lite
Based on the course glossary:

* **LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation):** A Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) method that freezes the pretrained model weights and represents weight updates using a low-rank product of two small matrices. Only these small matrices are trained, which drastically reduces the number of trainable parameters and produces small adapter files.
* **QLoRA (Quantized LoRA):** Builds upon LoRA by quantizing the base-model weights (for example, down to 4 bits) to significantly save memory. This allows you to train larger models on smaller GPUs.

To think about how they work together: LoRA focuses on *how* we represent and train the weight updates efficiently, whereas QLoRA adds *quantization* to the base weights beforehand to drastically lower the memory footprint during training.
