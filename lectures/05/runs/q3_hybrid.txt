Based on Lecture 03 (slide 3), foundation model properties from Lecture 02 serve as design assumptions for agents. Here is how each property maps to the agent design components:

1. **"Same question, different answer each time"**
   * *Agent Impact:* The same request leads to different steps each time, and errors can pile up.
   * *Handled by:* **Part 3 (Loop)**

2. **"Answers plausibly even when it doesn't know"**
   * *Agent Impact:* It may call nonexistent functions or misread tool results.
   * *Handled by:* **Part 8 (Failure)**

3. **"Knowledge has a cutoff"**
   * *Agent Impact:* Requires fetching new information.
   * *Handled by:* **Part 4 (Tools)**

4. **"Can't treat long context evenly"**
   * *Agent Impact:* In long tasks, earlier instructions and history get missed.
   * *Handled by:* **Part 6 (Memory)**

5. **"Can't tell text in the input from instructions"**
   * *Agent Impact:* Instructions found inside pages or files it reads can mistakenly trigger tool use.
   * *Handled by:* **Part 4 & 9 (Tools & Trust boundaries)**

6. **"Tells you what you want to hear (sycophancy)"**
   * *Agent Impact:* Reports "Done!" even when it didn't actually work.
   * *Handled by:* **Part 7 & 8 (Stopping & Failure)**

*(Additionally, the model's ability to **generate outputs in a fixed format** is handled by **Part 5 (Structured output)** to reliably emit tool calls.)*
