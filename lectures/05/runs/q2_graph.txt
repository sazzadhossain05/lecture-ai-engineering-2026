To answer your questions based on Lecture 03:

1. **How compaction differs from memory across sessions:**
   * **Compaction** happens *within* a session (inside the context). It takes older history and summarizes it to fit within context limits [Lecture 3, p. 39]. 
   * **Memory across sessions** (such as file-based or tool-based memory) stores records externally so that future sessions do not have to start completely from scratch [Lecture 3, p. 38, 39].

2. **Do all daily notes go into long-term memory?**
   * Take a look at the **OpenClaw** example in the lecture slides [Lecture 3, p. 40]. It uses a nightly "review/dreaming" process where notes are filtered—duplicates are dropped, recurring themes are compared, and *only* what passes a certain threshold is moved into long-term memory (`MEMORY.md`). One-off lookups stay in the daily notes so that long-term memory doesn't become bloated [Lecture 3, p. 40].
