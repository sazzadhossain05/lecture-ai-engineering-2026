   - *Agent Effect:* Calls nonexistent functions; misreads tool results.
   - *Handled by:* **Part 8 (Failure)**.

3. **"Knowledge has a cutoff"** 
   - *Agent Effect:* Needs to fetch new information.
   - *Handled by:* **Part 4 (Tools)**.

4. **"Can't treat long context evenly"** 
   - *Agent Effect:* Earlier instructions and history get missed in long tasks.
   - *Handled by:* **Part 6 (Memory)**.

5. **"Can't tell text in the input from instructions"** 
   - *Agent Effect:* Instructions found inside pages or files it reads trigger unwanted tool usage.
   - *Handled by:* **Part 4 & 9 (Trust boundaries)**.

6. **"Tells you what you want to hear (sycophancy)"** 
   - *Agent Effect:* Reports "Done!" even when it didn't actually work.
   - *Handled by:* **Part 7 & 8 (Stopping & failure)**.
